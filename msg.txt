Delete the notebook with its link, and schedule through the Fabric REST API

The scheduling never worked because it used the wrong API. The
workloadClient.itemSchedule client targets custom workload item types
declared in a manifest; a Notebook is a first-party Fabric item whose
schedules live in the REST API. The two contracts share no fields:
jobDefinitionObjectId does not exist in REST, and there is no Hourly
type at all.

Schedules now go to /jobs/RunNotebook/schedules directly, with a
Fabric-audience token. Three details decided whether the call was
accepted: localTimeZoneId must be a Windows identifier rather than
IANA, startDateTime and endDateTime are required in practice despite
being optional in the schema, and the timestamps carry no trailing Z.
The editor now offers the five cadences the portal offers, with Hourly
expressed as a Cron interval in minutes.

Run now returned 202 Accepted and we only accepted 200, which is why
every run reported failure.

Deleting a link now deletes its notebook. When that call fails the link
is still removed and the message says the notebook remains, rather than
claiming a cascade that did not happen.
